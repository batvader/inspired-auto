Who’s working on your car

Dan Gamble - Owner/Operator

My passion for cars started with building my own vehicles, getting involved in the street racing scene, and attending local car shows and gatherings. What started as a hobby grew into a dream of one day making a living doing what I love. After years of working on cars, I've finally turned that dream into reality by opening my own shop, where I can share my passion for cars through quality work and helping others bring their automotive projects to life.
