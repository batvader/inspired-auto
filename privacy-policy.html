Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Inspired Auto ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting our website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is handled when you interact with our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a booking or service inquiry through our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Vehicle year, make, and model
  • Requested automotive service
  • Additional information or comments you voluntarily provide

Please do not submit payment information, passwords, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through our website forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our website is used for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Reviewing service and appointment requests.
  • Contacting you regarding your inquiry.
  • Discussing your vehicle's reported issues and requested services.
  • Determining scheduling availability.
  • Following up on questions or requests.
  • Maintaining business records related to customer inquiries.

Submitting a booking request does not automatically establish an appointment or create an agreement for automotive repair services.

3. Third-Party Form Processing

Our website uses Formspree to process and deliver booking and service inquiry submissions.

When you submit information through our website, that information is transmitted to Formspree and forwarded to the shop owner for review and follow-up.

Formspree operates as an independent third-party service provider and may process or retain submitted information in accordance with its own privacy and data handling practices.

You can review Formspree's privacy information at Formspree's Privacy Policy .

4. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party behavioral analytics tools.

Our website hosting provider and other technical service providers may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and maintain the website.

Third-party services, including Formspree, may use their own technologies in connection with providing their services.

5. Information Sharing

Inspired Auto does not sell or rent personal information submitted through its website.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process inquiries, communicate with customers, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our business, customers, or legal rights.

6. Information Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website.

However, no method of electronic transmission, storage, or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain business records, fulfill legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Information handled by third-party providers may also be subject to their respective retention policies.

7. Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate business recordkeeping obligations.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided on our website.

8. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or third-party services. Inspired Auto is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites that we do not operate.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy as our business practices, website functionality, or applicable legal requirements change.

Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
