Warranty Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

This Warranty Policy explains the current warranty coverage offered by Inspired Auto for automotive parts and services performed at the shop.

1. Shop-Provided Labor Warranty

Inspired Auto does not currently offer a separate shop-provided warranty on labor or installation services. Customers should review the terms of their repair authorization and any applicable parts warranty before approving work.

2. Customer-Supplied Parts

Customers may supply their own parts for installation when accepted by Inspired Auto.

Inspired Auto does not provide a parts warranty or labor warranty on customer-supplied parts or the installation of those parts. Any manufacturer's or seller's warranty on customer-supplied parts must be handled directly with the applicable manufacturer or seller.

3. Parts Supplied by Inspired Auto

Parts purchased and supplied by Inspired Auto may be covered by a manufacturer's or supplier's parts warranty.

Coverage, duration, exclusions, and claim requirements are determined by the applicable manufacturer or supplier. Inspired Auto does not provide additional labor coverage under these parts warranties.

Customers should contact Inspired Auto if they believe a shop-supplied part may qualify for warranty consideration. The part may need to be inspected, returned, or submitted for review under the manufacturer's or supplier's claim process.

4. Warranty Limitations

Parts warranties may exclude failures caused by improper use, installation outside the manufacturer's requirements, modifications, misuse, accidents, neglect, or other conditions identified in the applicable warranty terms.

The availability of warranty coverage is determined by the applicable manufacturer or supplier. A parts warranty does not automatically cover related labor, diagnostic time, towing, rental vehicles, or other incidental expenses.

5. Warranty Questions and Claims

Customers with questions about a part supplied by Inspired Auto should contact the shop. Please provide the vehicle information, date of service, and a description of the issue.

Warranty claims remain subject to the applicable manufacturer's or supplier's requirements and approval process.

6. Rights Under Applicable Law

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive, exclude, or limit any warranty, right, or remedy that cannot legally be waived or limited under applicable Michigan or federal law.

7. Policy Changes

Inspired Auto may update this policy as its business practices change. The current version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
